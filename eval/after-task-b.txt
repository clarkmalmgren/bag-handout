What the targets did and did not reach
- hull overlap: target < 0.25, reached (0.155 at seed 1, 0.190 mean over 4 seeds, from 0.964).
- longest loop: target < 1.6 x mean, 1.62 at seed 1 (1.47 mean over seeds 1-4). The one long group
  covers the spread-out south end; raising weights.maxRoute to 2 shortens it to 7711 m but pushes
  hull overlap back up to ~0.66, so the trade was not taken.
- split street names: target <= 6, reached 7 (from 8). With 6 groups there are at least 5 internal
  boundaries, and on this map most of them have to fall part-way along a street rather than at a
  junction (S Mill Creek Dr alone runs the length of the subdivision), so 5-7 is close to the floor.
  compact = 0 splits marginally fewer streets but interleaves the groups much more (overlap 0.86).

Two seed rewrites were implemented, measured and rejected, both much worse than the plain balanced
k-means seed that ships (hull overlap after annealing, mean of 4 seeds):
- region growing over the walking-distance link graph (balanced, one connected region per group):
  2.16 with straight-line growth priority, 2.45 with walking-distance priority, vs 0.19 shipped.
- k-means plus fragment repair (small walking-disconnected pieces handed to the neighbouring group
  they touch): 2.34. Both fail for the same reason: a house that is walking-far from its group but
  spatially inside it (cul-de-sac ends, houses backing onto each other) gets pushed to a group that
  is nearer on foot but elsewhere on the map, which is exactly the mixing the user objects to.

Contiguity guard: 50 m was measured and is not usable on this data. The door-to-door distance to a
house's nearest neighbour is 27 m (p50) but 58 m (p90), so below about 70 m a boundary house has no
house of the receiving group within the link distance, every move adds a component, the guard
rejects it, and the annealer stops moving (solve time drops to 0.3 s and the result is the seed).
80 m is the tightest value that keeps the chain moving; it halves the hull overlap versus the old
150 m (0.19 vs 0.52 mean) and the guard still earns its place (0.19 with the guard vs 0.57 with it
switched off).
